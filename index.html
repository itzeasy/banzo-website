Frequently asked questions

How do I book a tour package with Banzo?

Send us an enquiry through the form, a call or WhatsApp. We share a day-wise itinerary and a quote in rupees; once you approve it, a part payment confirms your hotels and cab and the balance is due before you travel.

Are package prices inclusive of GST?

The prices shown are indicative starting prices per person on twin sharing and exclude GST. GST is added as per government rules and shown separately on your invoice.

Can I customise a package?

Yes. Every itinerary can be changed — add or remove days, upgrade hotels, combine two destinations or switch to a private, slower-paced plan for elders and children.

Do packages include flights or train tickets?

Packages are land-only by default so you can travel from any city. We can book flights or trains for you on request.

Which payment methods can I use?

You can pay by UPI, net banking, debit or credit card, or bank transfer. Every payment is acknowledged with a receipt.

What if I need to cancel?

Charges depend on how close to the travel date you cancel. Read our Cancellation & Refund Policy for the full details.
